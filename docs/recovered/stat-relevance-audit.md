## Full DPS relevance audit (v1 — 2026-10-22)

**Interpretation rule:** “No default DPS value” means that a stat does not improve the modeled offensive output under the product's stated normal-case assumptions (the player survives and attacks land). It does **not** mean the stat is universally useless in the game. Stats may matter in another mode, at a threshold, for a specific skill, or for a separate survival/utility objective. Do not let CP weights decide DPS relevance.

Nexon's guide explicitly separates its CP calculation from combat damage, documents multiple aggregation rules and caps, and warns that actual outcomes vary by content adjustments, skill characteristics, and battle conditions. Its generic damage sequence also has exceptions, so all classifications below are context-sensitive and versioned. The English landing page's embedded Stat Dictionary is not fully extractable in this research pass; formula-derived terms are marked as such rather than claiming this is an exhaustive current item-option catalog.

### Full-audit implementation rules

1. Use a stat registry with separate fields for **CP contribution**, **outgoing damage**, **hit rate**, **cadence/uptime**, **incoming damage/survival**, **multi-target throughput**, and **utility/progression**. “CP stat” must never be treated as synonymous with “DPS stat.”
2. Each stat needs a relevance predicate (target type, skill type, target state, cap, effective hit/crit rate, rotation, or encounter). If the predicate is false, its marginal DPS contribution is exactly zero in that modeled scenario.
3. Compute all deltas on top of the confirmed current baseline and apply caps/stacking before comparing items. Do not assign arbitrary tie-breaker weights to unmodeled stats.
4. Label evidence per rule: **official combat formula**, **official CP-only formula**, **screenshot observation**, or **unverified inference**. Keep uncertain OCR labels such as Stat Prop. Damage and Damage Taken out of the score until resolved.
5. Official generic formula exceptions matter: some attacks skip hit/crit/content steps; some use target Max HP, fixed damage, defense-ignoring damage, or other special rules. A stat is not globally meaningless merely because it is inactive for one scenario.

### Confidence notes / unresolved rules

- Critical Rate above 100%: standard PvE overflow behavior is **unknown** from the official material reviewed. The 100% cap applies to a specific PvP correction weighting only. Do not call overflow wasted or award benefit beyond a guessed cap until combat documentation or controlled testing settles it.
- Accuracy: no universal Accuracy cap was verified. The guide gives a specific 70% enemy evasion result/cap when enemy Evasion is at least 100 above Accuracy; a scenario hit-rate result still needs target Evasion, attack eligibility, and the curve below that gap.
- Stat Prop. Damage and Damage Taken: retain the screenshot's raw wording; neither is safe to normalize from label alone.
- Attack Speed, cooldown reduction, skill levels, buff duration, and companions require skill/preset mechanics; their presence in CP does not establish their DPS value or weight.
- “No default DPS value” is scenario-specific: HP, Defense, Evasion, Damage Taken reduction, and similar defensive stats can matter to survival or PvP receiver-side rules. EXP and Meso are progression/economy goals. Keep these dimensions separate from the default outgoing-DPS recommendation.
