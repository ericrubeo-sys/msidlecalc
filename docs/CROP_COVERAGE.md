# Advisory crop coverage

The user's iPhone follow-up shows a reviewed second-area boundary inside the stat-label column while the numbers remain inside. This is direct evidence of excluded pixels, not evidence that OCR failed to recognize labels which were present in its input. These attachments show the application and are not fresh original-game accuracy fixtures.

`public/domain/crop-coverage.js` provides pure `assessCropCoverage({blocks,width,height,scale,region})`. It examines word boxes from the initial whole-image recognition and the actual selected region in original image pixels. It returns an array of advisory issues or `[]` when evidence is insufficient. An empty array does **not** certify complete coverage or accurate OCR.

Each issue contains `version`, `code` (`possibleClippedLabels` or `possibleClippedValues`), `side`, `coordinateSystem`, the unchanged `region`, and `rows`. Rows contain only `labelBounds`, `valueBounds`, and `outsideBounds`, each represented as `{x,y,width,height}` in original image pixels. No recognized text, numeric values, suggested replacement labels or automatic crop expansion is returned. `public/ocr-reader.js` attaches these as each panel's `coverageIssues`, with `coverageProvenance` identifying the initial whole-image observation, its PSM and scale, selected region, version and advisory status. The warning does not confirm any item, line, value or role.

The bounded heuristic requires at least two distinct vertical rows with mutually aligned local letter-bearing label fragments and complete numeric tokens. Unicode letter labels are allowed without a stat dictionary; labels containing digits may be missed rather than repaired. A left warning requires label pixels outside the left edge while a whole numeric token is inside. A right warning requires a local label inside and numeric pixels beyond the right edge. Nearby pairs must overlap vertically by at least 65% of the larger word height; gaps and offsets are bounded relative to crop width; repeated rows must support similar label/value columns. Headings alone, one row, duplicate observations of one row, distant inventory text, invalid geometry and absent word evidence do not suffice. Only horizontal clipping is considered; no guarantee is made about omitted top/bottom rows.

The closest local letter-bearing fragment before a value must itself cross or fall outside the left edge; a complete inside label prevents borrowing a nearby outside label. This deliberately misses some partially excluded multiword labels. A right-side numeric observation does not cause a warning when another complete numeric token is already inside on that row: the outside token may be the game's displayed delta.

Limitations: initial whole-image OCR may itself omit or misread words; neighboring interface text can share plausible geometry; restrictive bounds may miss real clipping. Warnings are opportunities for the user to check a crop, not instructions to auto-expand it. Do not copy labels from the equipped item: the potential new equip can have different, missing or repeated stats. Unknown observations and original transcripts remain available for correction. This heuristic does not improve or validate OCR recognition accuracy by itself.

Verification: `tests/crop-coverage.test.mjs` uses invented geometry for excluded labels, fully included rows, Unicode/unknown labels, right-edge numeric clipping, scaled coordinates, headings, adjacent inventory, duplicated rows, invalid geometry, bounded evidence and reader integration. It asserts immutable selected crops and unconfirmed numerical state. These are deterministic implementation tests, not live iPhone gesture tests or a measured game-image accuracy estimate.
