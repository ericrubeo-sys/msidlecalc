Sources & current limitations

No damage formula has been validated for this preview. No Attack, Main Stat, Critical Rate, Critical Damage, Accuracy, or skill-level scaling is assumed. Activity is recorded as context and does not change raw differences.

This first form covers six stat lines, not every possible item effect. A higher number in the table is not an equip recommendation.

Privacy & local data

Item inputs and your optional screenshot stay in the current page session. This app sends neither to a processing service and does not save them across reloads. Only your appearance preference is saved in this browser. There are no accounts or app analytics. Hosting providers may process routine request information to serve the site.
